### 1. Actor: Job Seeker (Candidate)
*Focus: Finding the right job, applying efficiently, and managing their professional brand.*

**Account & Profile Management**
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** sign up using my email, Google, or LinkedIn account **so that** I can create a profile quickly without remembering another password.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** upload my resume in multiple formats (PDF, Word, Text) **so that** the system can auto-parse my work history and skills to pre-fill my profile.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** toggle my profile visibility (Public/Private) **so that** recruiters can find me in searches, but I can remain hidden from my current employer.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** save multiple versions of my resume and cover letters **so that** I can tailor my application to different industries or job types.

**Job Discovery & Search**
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** search for jobs using keywords, job titles, and company names **so that** I can find roles that match my specific expertise.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** filter results by location, distance (e.g., within 25 miles), salary range, date posted, and employment type (Remote/Hybrid/On-site/Contract) **so that** I only see opportunities that fit my lifestyle needs.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** save my search criteria and set up email/SMS alerts **so that** I am instantly notified when new matching jobs are posted without having to re-enter my filters.

**Application Process**
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** apply to a job using the "Easy Apply" feature with my default resume **so that** I can submit applications in one click for time-sensitive roles.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** answer custom screening questions (e.g., "Do you have 5+ years in Python?") embedded in the application form **so that** employers can pre-qualify me before reviewing my full CV.
- **As a** job seeker, **I want to** view a centralized history of all my submitted applications **so that** I can track which roles are pending, viewed, or rejected.

### 2. Actor: Employer / Recruiter / Hiring Manager
*Focus: Attracting talent, filtering candidates, and streamlining the hiring pipeline.*

**Account & Billing Management**
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** register my company and complete a business verification process **so that** I can establish trust and legitimacy on the platform.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** choose and manage a subscription plan (pay-per-job, monthly subscription, or bulk postings) **so that** I can control my recruitment budget.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** manage multiple sub-accounts for my team members (with varying permissions) **so that** different HR staff can handle different departments without overlapping.

**Job Posting & Promotion**
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** create a detailed job posting using a structured template (with rich text and bullet points) **so that** I clearly communicate responsibilities and qualifications.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** sponsor or "promote" a job listing for a set daily budget **so that** it appears at the top of search results and receives higher visibility.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** pause, re-post, or close a job listing instantly **so that** I don't receive new applications once the position is filled or on hold.

**Talent Sourcing (Resume Database)**
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** search the extensive resume database using Boolean operators (AND/OR/NOT) and filters (years of experience, education, skills) **so that** I can proactively headhunt passive candidates.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** save these candidate searches and set up alerts **so that** I am notified when new resumes matching my criteria are uploaded to the database.

**Applicant Tracking & Pipeline Management**
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** view all incoming applications in a single dashboard with sorting options (by date, relevance score, or skills match) **so that** I can prioritize the most qualified candidates.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** move candidates through customizable pipeline stages (e.g., New -> Screening -> Interview -> Offer -> Hired) **so that** I can track the progress of my hiring funnels.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** send bulk emails (rejections or interview invitations) using pre-written templates **so that** I can communicate efficiently with hundreds of applicants.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** leave internal notes and rate candidates within their profiles **so that** my team members can collaborate and review feedback before making a decision.

**Analytics & ROI**
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** view analytics on my job posts (number of views, clicks, and applications) **so that** I can measure the effectiveness of my job description and paid promotions.
- **As a** recruiter, **I want to** see a breakdown of applicant demographics (e.g., location, current role, skills) **so that** I can assess if my job ad is attracting the right geographical or skill-based audience.

### 3. Actor: System Administrator (Internal Monster Team)
*Focus: Platform integrity, security, content moderation, and technical maintenance.*

**User & Compliance Management**
- **As an** admin, **I want to** review and approve new employer accounts **so that** I can verify business legitimacy and prevent fraudulent or scam companies from using the platform.
- **As an** admin, **I want to** temporarily suspend or permanently ban users/employers who violate the Terms of Service **so that** the community remains safe and trustworthy.
- **As an** admin, **I want to** manage GDPR/CCPA data deletion requests **so that** the platform remains compliant with global data privacy regulations.

**Content Moderation**
- **As an** admin, **I want to** review job postings that have been flagged by the community or AI algorithms **so that** I can remove duplicate, misleading, or inappropriate job ads.
- **As an** admin, **I want to** curate and update the internal taxonomy (job categories, skill libraries, and industry tags) **so that** the search engine and AI matching algorithms remain accurate and up-to-date.

**Platform Operations & Configuration**
- **As an** admin, **I want to** monitor the health of the AI matching engine and search index **so that** I can ensure search results load quickly and relevantly for all users.
- **As an** admin, **I want to** configure system-wide thresholds (e.g., max daily applications per job, rate limits for API calls) **so that** the platform does not get overwhelmed by bot traffic or spam.
- **As an** admin, **I want to** oversee financial transactions and process refunds for employers **so that** billing disputes are resolved smoothly.

**Reporting & Insights**
- **As an** admin, **I want to** generate executive-level reports on platform metrics (total active users, job postings, revenue, and market demand trends) **so that** leadership can make strategic business decisions.
